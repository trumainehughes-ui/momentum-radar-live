How to Use Momentum Radar

Simple guide: choose what you want to see, then click a game when you want a full matchup breakdown.
1. Choose a SportUse the sport tabs to open the section you want to explore.
2. Game DaySee every game on the day's slate. Click a game name to open all available analytics for that matchup.
3. Inside a GameFind player analytics, injuries/inactives, and three Same Game Parlay ideas: Small, Medium, and Nuke. Use ← Back to Games when you're finished.
4. Top 10 Player ListsUse the player-category tabs to see the top players across the day's games.
5. Same Game ParlayWant an SGP for one matchup? Go to Game Day → click that game → Same Game Parlay.
6. Live TrackersDuring games, use the live tracker to follow results as they happen. A ⭐ is used only when a result matches a locked, verified pregame selection.
Reading the DataSportsbook lines and odds are supporting market information. They help you understand available bets; they are not guarantees. Always check the sportsbook before placing a wager.
