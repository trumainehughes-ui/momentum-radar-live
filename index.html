🪑 Momentum Radar — How to Use

New here? Start with a sport, choose a game, then explore the matchup and player categories. This guide explains the numbers, AI tools, and what is — and is not — a verified sportsbook selection.

1. Choose a sport and date⚾ MLB covers home-run opportunities. 🏈 NFL covers matchup analysis, player props and touchdown tracking. 🏀 NBA covers games, player stats and available SGP analysis. Select a game date to see that slate.
2. Open a gameChoose Game Day, then tap a matchup to see its own player projections, injury evidence, weather when relevant, and Same Game Parlay ideas. Use Back to Games to return to the slate.
3. Understand player categoriesPassing yards = QB yards through the air. Rushing yards = yards gained running. Receiving yards = yards on catches. Receptions = catches made. Anytime TD = player scores a touchdown. NBA: Points, Rebounds, Assists, 3-Pointers, and PRA (points + rebounds + assists).
4. Read rankings and probabilitiesTop 10 boards compare players within a category. A projected yardage number is an estimate, not a sportsbook line. A model probability is an estimate, not a guaranteed result. Check the matchup, opponent defense, recent usage and injury context before interpreting a pick.
5. Small, Medium and Nuke SGPsAn SGP combines selections from one game. Small targets roughly +2000 to +3000, Medium +3000 to +8000, and Nuke above +10000. These are target odds bands, not promised payouts. Higher potential returns generally mean more risk. Picks may mix passing, rushing, receiving, receptions and TDs when eligible.
6. Model candidate versus verified betMODEL_ONLY / MODEL_CANDIDATE means the AI generated an idea, but a sportsbook has not confirmed its combined SGP price. Verified book quote means the sportsbook returned a correlated combined price for those selections. DraftKings and FanDuel can have different markets, limits and prices. Never treat an unquoted model candidate as a ready-to-place ticket.
7. Injuries, starters and availabilityInjury and depth-chart checks help screen players. OUT, injured-reserve and otherwise ineligible players should not appear in active suggested legs. QUESTIONABLE means availability is uncertain, not confirmed. Always verify official game-day inactives and the current sportsbook offer.
8. Weather and matchup intelligenceFor outdoor NFL games, weather information can highlight wind, rain, snow or temperature that might affect passing, kicking and game flow. Matchup analysis compares each offense with the opposing defense, including player roles and defensive tendencies. Weather impact is an estimate, not a guaranteed change in performance.
9. What the AI features doData Quality Guardian flags stale or inconsistent inputs; Injury & Opportunity Radar tracks role changes; Prop Value Detector compares model estimates with available lines; Parlay Architect builds candidate combinations; Matchup Intelligence evaluates both sides; Game Script Simulator explores possible game flows; Breakout & Shadow Radar looks for less-obvious opportunities; Postgame Learning Lab reviews outcomes. A feature's name does not mean its output has been independently verified.
10. Live tracking, stars and historyLive trackers show in-game developments; history helps review hits, misses and previous picks. A ⭐ indicates a matching locked, verified selection where supported — not a guarantee. Final scores and settled bets can differ because sportsbook house rules apply.
11. Refresh schedule and freshnessPregame collection is designed for the day before, game day and a final check around 30 minutes before kickoff or tip-off. Injury or line changes may trigger a new assessment. Check the displayed timestamps and status: a scheduled refresh is not proof that current sportsbook prices were successfully retrieved.
12. A simple way to use Momentum RadarChoose sport → choose date → open matchup → review injuries, weather and opponent defense → compare player categories → inspect SGP status → confirm every leg and the combined price directly at your sportsbook before placing any bet. Odds and availability can change.

Momentum Radar provides analysis, not guaranteed winners. Bet only what you can afford to lose. Some NBA and sportsbook features may show model-only or limited data until verified markets are available.
