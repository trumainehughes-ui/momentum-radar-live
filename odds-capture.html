Capture public sportsbook odds

Works on your Android phone — no computer or Chrome companion required. Momentum Radar can read a screenshot of public sportsbook odds locally on your phone. You must review the extracted lines before saving.

  1. Choose the NFL matchup below.
  2. Open FanDuel or DraftKings on your phone, view the player's alternate yardage prices, and take a screenshot.
  3. Return here, select the screenshot, tap Read screenshot, review the exact prices, then tap Save.
  4. Open Momentum Radar or its public site in the same phone browser to see your current observations.

Saved observations expire after 20 minutes. Screenshots are processed on-device, not uploaded. This is a user-assisted workaround, not automatic sportsbook extraction or a bookmaker-quoted correlated SGP.
